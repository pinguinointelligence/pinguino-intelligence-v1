Community and account spines. This workstream must extend those spines; it must not build a
parallel admin catalog, referral ledger, subscription cache, invite-code system or recipe

## Authorities to reuse

- Product creation/version/retirement: canonical product ingest/lifecycle service and immutable
  product versions; no direct product inserts.
- Market availability: `product_variant_markets`; country of origin remains a product fact.
- Favorites/private overlays: `user_product_relations`.
- Access: `entitlements` plus Partner status and `admin_users`; do not infer from email.
- Billing: Stripe webhook event ledger and `customer_subscriptions`; never browser-authored state.
- Attribution/commission/refund/payout: existing referral and financial ledgers only.
- Community moderation: `community_reports`, publications and existing moderation RPCs; never private recipes.
- Audit: append-only `audit_log` plus domain-specific immutable event rows.
- Invite Home month: existing invite-code tables and entitlement source.

## Privacy boundary

The Admin read model may expose operational metadata needed for support and control. It must not
expose password material, provider/service keys, payment-card data, private non-shared recipe
bodies or exact private recipe grams. Product-request images remain private and must use the
existing evidence-retention boundary rather than becoming public catalog assets.
