# Main Flavour Envelope Registry

Registry authority is `product_behavior_policy_versions`; the TypeScript registry is a pure/test adapter, not the deployment authority.

Initial owner-provisional dairy seeds:

| Policy | Profile | Family / form | ECO | OPTIMAL | Hard | Basis | Carrier |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| generic fruit fresh/puree | fruit gelato | fruit / fresh or puree | 20% | 35% | 45% | fruit equivalent | 30% |
| berry fresh/puree | fruit gelato | berry / fresh or puree | 25% | 35% | 45% | fruit equivalent | 30% |
| kiwi fresh | fruit gelato | kiwi / fresh | 10% | 15% | 20% | fruit equivalent | 30% |
| banana fresh | fruit gelato | banana / fresh | 10% | 20% | 30% | fruit equivalent | 30% |
| pure nut paste | nut gelato | nut / pure nut paste | 8% | 15% | 15% | nut equivalent | 30% |

Sweetened/diluted nut compounds need an exact reviewed factor. Chocolate, coffee/tea, alcohol, Sorbet, Vegan and Protein envelopes remain `UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_REVIEW` until their form/basis and profile evidence are approved. This registry does not pretend those matrices are complete.

New reviewed taxonomy/policy rows can classify a future product server-side without a frontend deployment. Unknown rows create consolidated review cases.
