## Root cause

The exact-original underweight path was rejected at two layers:

1. `restoreOriginalProfile` returned no candidates whenever every physical amount was at or below the saved baseline (`scaleFactor === 1`).
2. Production Rescue then required a restore candidate to be strictly larger than the current canonical target, rejecting the exact saved target even if the Engine supplied it.

Banana overage did not enter either rejection: its physical fruit ratio required `scaleFactor > 1` and a final mass above the saved target. This is a same-version coverage defect, not bundle or deployment drift.
