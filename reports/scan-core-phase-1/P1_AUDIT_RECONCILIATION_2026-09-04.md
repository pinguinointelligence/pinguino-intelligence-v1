## §17 — rectified evidence rule (conservative, corpus-derived)
The audit decodes on the rectified crop by default and would fast-lane two agreeing rectified decodes within 400 ms; the Note10+ can produced six consecutive rectified aliases (`0141200001098`, frames 139–166) that satisfy that rule. Rule now: a rectified read may **confirm only together with at least one agreeing read from a non-rectified crop of the same track**; rectified-only agreement never confirms, however many frames repeat it (a systematic homography alias repeats identically). Rectified-only tracks stay READING with a "hold / move slightly" request that changes the geometry. To be re-examined with geometry-diversity evidence once the corpus is recovered.
