# Fantasy 50 — primary-source research and generation gate

**Audit date / source retrieval date:** 2026-08-15

**Scope:** all 50 concepts in the authoritative working list, in its required category and priority order.
**State:** research only; non-production; owner review required.

## Repository finding and guardrails

No Fantasy 50 research registry or executable Fantasy template set exists in the repository. `src/data/recipes/flavorCatalogue.generated.ts` includes brand-inspired discovery cards, and `reports/MAPPER_2088_PRODUCT_BEHAVIOR_AUDIT.csv` includes a few products/inclusions, but neither supplies a versioned frozen formula, approved canonical vector, separate base/topping grams, process, technical result or sensory proof. In particular, Mapper inclusion rows such as Snickers `PI-ING-001988`, Ferrero `PI-ING-002002`, Raffaello `PI-ING-002003` and Kinder Bueno `PI-ING-002004` are topping-only product evidence, not permission to require those products or copy their formulas.

- Every public name below is an **owner-review working name** and remains `TRADEMARK_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
- The original reference is **owner-only metadata**. It must not appear in normal customer search or UI without separate approval.
- Manufacturer labels/pages support only the declared sensory architecture, ingredients, percentages and allergens of the cited market product. They do not disclose a proprietary formula and do not justify an ice-cream formula.
- All source summaries are paraphrases. PINGÜINO base/topping profiles are internal concept decisions and are explicitly separate from source facts.
- A library recipe must not require the original packaged product or reproduce its protected get-up. Generic canonical ingredients and independent formulation are required.
- All 50 rows remain `RESEARCH · NIEZWERYFIKOWANE PRODUKCYJNIE · WYMAGA TESTU`. No executable grams are approved.

Every `Recipe status` cell spells out the research/test state and records any additional source, alcohol or vector blocker. No row has a canonical recipe/version, approved ingredient vector, technical proof, sensory test or publication decision.
