# Lost & Legendary — global research

Date: 2026-08-08

Scope: research and feasibility candidates only. This file does not contain production formulas or grams.

## Method and evidence standard

The sweep prioritised primary digitised cookbooks, official cultural institutions, academic work and official registries. A historical mention alone is not enough: the product must also pass Mapper, formulation, Engine, kitchen and sensory gates before publication. Folklore such as “Marco Polo brought ice cream”, an exact ancient age for faloodeh, or a single named “inventor” of kulfi was deliberately not promoted as fact.

Claude Code completed read-only streams for Europe, Middle East / North Africa, and South / East / Southeast Asia. The remaining Claude streams (Americas, Oceania/Africa outside MENA, Natural Icons, feasibility and UX) hit the local Claude session limit. Codex added one official-source Americas candidate, Queso helado arequipeño. Missing sweeps are labelled `RESEARCH_REQUIRED`; no output was invented to fill them.

## Legal and authenticity guard

- “Pistacchio Verde di Bronte DOP” is reserved for compliant certified supply and labelling. Otherwise PINGÜINO uses a neutral pistachio name. Official specification: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52016XC1101%2805%29
- “Maraş” and other protected/regional identifiers must not be used for a konjac-based reconstruction. The UI calls it an adaptation.
- Wild orchid salep raises conservation and lawful-sourcing concerns. A substitute changes aroma and identity and must never be silent.
- Shared traditions stay shared. The runtime field may name multiple countries/regions rather than arbitrarily assigning ownership.
