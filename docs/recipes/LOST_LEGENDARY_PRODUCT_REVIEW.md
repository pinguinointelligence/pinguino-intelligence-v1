## Customer-family decision

- `Proteinowe` remains valid only as the existing product type/filter.
- Internal clustering may retain `protein`, `aromatic`, `other`, `dessert` and other analytical buckets.
- Customer discovery resolves technical rows back to a concrete flavour family when the actual source language supports one.
- Primary suggestions are selected dynamically by source popularity from concrete families only, capped at six; they are not the six largest clusters.
- Customer search/browse does not promote `Proteinowe`, `Inne` or `Aromatyczne` as primary flavour-family cards.

## Publication decision

The seven-stage gate is unchanged:

`RESEARCHED → MAPPER_READY → FORMULATED → ENGINE_VERIFIED → KITCHEN_TESTED → SENSORY_APPROVED → PUBLISHED`

Normal customer visibility still requires `PUBLISHED`, authentic/adaptable status, a supported non-special product type, workbench compatibility and zero unavailable Mapper items. Owner review reuses the existing review-mode contract: local DEV or staging explicitly enabled with `VITE_DESIGN_REVIEW=1`, plus a resolved Pro owner/QA persona. Pre-publication cards receive strong PINK state labels and never appear production-ready.
