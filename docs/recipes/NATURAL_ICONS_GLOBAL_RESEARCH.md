## Naming guard

“Bronte DOP”, “Hokkaido”, or any other protected/geographically specific qualifier is not used merely as a style word. `Pistacchio Puro` and `Latte Puro` are intentionally neutral. If certified supply and legal labelling later support a protected name, that is a separate approval.

Official Bronte PDO specification: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52016XC1101%2805%29

## Publication state

All 10 directions are pre-publication. Six can carry existing canonical ingredient IDs into the intent handoff, but none has yet passed formulation, Engine verification, kitchen testing or sensory approval. Therefore public count is **0** and every development card is pink.
