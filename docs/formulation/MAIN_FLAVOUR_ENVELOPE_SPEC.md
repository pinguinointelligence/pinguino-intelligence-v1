# Main Flavour Envelope Specification

Main is a protected flavor identity and objective, not a grams-lock alias. The existing Main set/ratio contract remains the identity authority. This product layer adds versioned eligibility and concentration-aware bounds without changing Engine formulas.

## Policy values

Every Main-capable binding requires:

- stable family/subfamily/form;
- profile;
- basis;
- ECO floor;
- OPTIMAL ceiling;
- absolute hard limit;
- equivalent factor;
- policy/taxonomy/classifier versions;
- evidence state.

Database constraints enforce `0 <= floor <= ceiling <= hard <= 100` and a positive equivalent factor.

## Evaluation

`equivalent grams = planned grams × reviewed equivalent factor`.

The total same-family Main group is compared with the one policy envelope while the existing exact ratios are preserved. A 30% pistachio compound therefore contributes 0.30 equivalent grams per retained gram; it is never treated as pure paste.

ECO may optimize cost only after safety, identity, locks, ratio, floor and technical quality. Missing price is unknown, never zero. OPTIMAL ignores price, holds the best Engine-safe score class, and searches Main only up to the policy ceiling. The hard limit applies in every mode.

## Multi-Main

- Same-family: total equivalent group receives one floor/ceiling/hard limit.
- Mixed-family: every family pairing must be explicitly approved. Otherwise `multi_main_policy_unknown`.
- Ratio remains the accepted 1:1 / 2:1 / 1:1:1 contract.

## Dairy carrier

Owner-provisional ordinary dairy policies require at least 30% exact approved liquid dairy carrier. The initial exact carrier list contains liquid milks only; cream, condensed milk, powders and name heuristics are not silently accepted. The boundary is checked on the whole-gram recipe. Protein policies do not inherit the ordinary dairy rule unless their own policy says so.

## Fail closed

Unknown form, concentration, ABV, retained-mass basis or profile policy leaves Standard eligibility independent but blocks Main/automatic optimization. Stabilizer dosage remains its separate accepted contract.
