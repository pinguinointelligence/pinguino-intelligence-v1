## Cross-market implementation limits

- Engine nutrition is a calculation source, not a complete US/Canada/AU-NZ legal panel.
- Package net quantity is not batch mass.
- Legal product name cannot be inferred safely from the recipe display name.
- Ingredient percentages are not universally required and may reveal unnecessary formulation detail; selective QUID/market logic remains to implement.
- Empty allergen text is unknown, not “none”. Precautionary allergen labelling requires a real cross-contact risk assessment.
- Ingredient `shelf_life_days` does not establish final-product shelf life.
- Concrete profile verification must bind product type, package, language, market and profile version.
