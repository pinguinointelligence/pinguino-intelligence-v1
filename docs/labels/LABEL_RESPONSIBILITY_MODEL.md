# Label responsibility model

Checked: 2026-08-09

## Truthful in-product copy

Use this concise pre-print statement:

> Etykieta została przygotowana na podstawie danych receptury, produkcji i informacji podanych przez użytkownika.

Optional clarification in the editor:

> PINGÜINO wspiera przygotowanie danych i sprawdzenie kompletności. Nie jest organem regulacyjnym ani certyfikatem prawnym.

Do not print a giant disclaimer on the consumer label.

## Division of responsibility

PINGÜINO is responsible for:

- traceability from completed actual batch to Master Label;
- preserving the selected market profile/version and sources;
- not fabricating allergens, shelf life, net package quantity or legal names;
- presenting missing and partial data honestly;
- keeping internal notes/costs out of the print model;
- deterministic preflight and system-print preparation.

The food business/operator remains responsible for:

- correctness and completeness of ingredient/product metadata supplied to the system;
- legal product identity and claims;
- facility/operator/address data;
- validated shelf-life basis;
- actual packaging/net-fill data;
- cross-contact risk assessment and precautionary allergen wording;
- final review for the concrete product, market, language and package;
- retaining evidence and following applicable local law.

An acknowledgement confirms review; it does not transfer or erase legal responsibility.

## Prohibited claims

The product must not say:

- “legally certified”;
- “approved by the regulator”;
- “compliant worldwide”;
- “allergen-free” from missing/empty evidence;
- “best before” derived from an unvalidated arithmetic rule.

## Publication state

- `VERIFIED`: reserved for a concrete, versioned profile after legal/product/package validation.
- `PARTIAL`: authoritative baseline researched, renderer/product overlays incomplete.
- `RESEARCH_REQUIRED`: no verified market guidance; custom editing remains available with a pink warning.

The current EU, US, Canada, UK and AU/NZ implementations are `PARTIAL`; all other markets are `RESEARCH_REQUIRED`.
